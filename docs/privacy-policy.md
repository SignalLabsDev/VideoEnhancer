## Video Enhancer Privacy Policy

- Video Enhancer does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.
- The extension saves your display preferences locally using Chrome's storage API so they persist between visits. This includes the enabled state, sharpen, contrast, saturation, brightness, gamma, compatibility and overlay settings, panel visibility and position, and any named presets you save.
- Backup & restore writes a JSON file to your downloads only when you click Export. The file stays on your device; the extension never uploads it.
- No data leaves your browser and nothing is shared with third parties.
